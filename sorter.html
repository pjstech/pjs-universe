PJ SORTER

The smart photo rating and sorting app built by a photographer, for photographers. Rate your shots fast, sort the keepers, ship your best work.

What It Does

SORT FASTER.
SHOOT BETTER.

After every race day you come home with hundreds of shots. PJ Sorter helps you cut through them fast — rate, tag, sort, and export your best work in a fraction of the time.

⚡
Rate in Seconds
Keyboard-driven rating system lets you blaze through hundreds of photos at speed. One key press per photo — keep or cull.
📂
Smart Sorting
Automatically organises your rated photos into folders. Your 5-stars ready to export, your rejects out of the way.
🏎️
Built for Race Photography
Designed around the workflow of a motorsport photographer. Series tagging, event labelling, batch delivery — all built in.
🔒
Works Offline
No internet required. Works on your laptop in the car park right after the race. Your photos stay on your machine.
📊
Shoot Analytics
See your keeper rate, your best events, your most consistent sessions. Learn from your data and improve every race day.
🎯
AI-Assisted Rating
Optional AI suggestions flag your sharpest, best-composed shots first. You make the final call — AI just saves you time.
