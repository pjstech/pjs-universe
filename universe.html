SHOOT & BUILD — THE FULL STORY

One car. Two perspectives. The real thing captured at speed — then recreated on my workbench. This is the story nobody else is telling.

01

I travel to international events — WEC, Porsche Cup, Le Mans — and photograph the real car at speed. Every angle, every detail.
02
