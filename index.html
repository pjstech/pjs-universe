The real story

I always wanted my passions to unite under one flag — so that one day, the thing I love could also fuel my dreams.

May 2026
The dream takes shape

Being a family man takes a large portion of any dream away — and I made peace with that. But in May 2026 I started building anyway. A portfolio. A brand. An app to help the motorsport community. The first real bricks of something that could one day stand on its own.

June 2026
Everything changed

Then disaster struck. One of our children was diagnosed with ALL — acute lymphoblastic leukemia. Our world collapsed right then and there.

Overnight, the dream wasn't about fueling a future anymore. It became about fueling something far more important.

Today
The fight

So this is where we are. The same hands that build scale models and frame race cars at full speed are now working toward one thing: our son's recovery.

Same passion.

How you can walk with us

No donations, no charity asks — just honest work for a real reason. If you've ever wanted a hand-built replica or trackside photography, this is the moment it means the most.
